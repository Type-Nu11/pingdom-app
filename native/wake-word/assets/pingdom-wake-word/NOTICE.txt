LOCAL KEYWORD CONFIGURATION
keywords.txt is added by Pingdom. It registers the entire phrase Hey Pingdy
with the application alias Hey_Pingdy. HH EY1 P IH1 NG D IY0 is a manually
specified English pronunciation for the proper name, not a verified acoustic
claim. Korean-accented pronunciation, false-positive/negative rates and device
performance have not been verified by adding this configuration.
